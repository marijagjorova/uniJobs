# uniJobs

UniJobs is a university job and internship management platform designed to connect students with employers. The platform allows students to browse and apply for opportunities, while employers can publish job postings and manage applications. University administrators can oversee users, postings, and platform activity through an administration panel. The system aims to simplify communication and improve career opportunities for students.

## Functional Areas
- Student registration and login
- Employer registration and login
- Job and internship posting management
- Job application system
- Search and filtering of opportunities
- Administration and user management
